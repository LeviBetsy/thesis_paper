\section{Camera}

The camera is modeled as an ideal pinhole with intrinsic matrix
\begin{equation}
  K = \begin{bmatrix}
        f_x & 0   & c_x \\
        0   & f_y & c_y \\
        0   & 0   & 1
      \end{bmatrix},
  \label{eq:intrinsics}
\end{equation}
where $f_x, f_y$ are the focal lengths in pixels and $(c_x, c_y)$ is the principal point.
